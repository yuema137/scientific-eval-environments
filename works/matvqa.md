## Overview

MatVQA challenges multimodal LLMs on visual-scientific reasoning in materials science: 1,325 questions across four structure-property-performance reasoning tasks over real materials imagery (microscopy, diffraction patterns), with textual shortcuts iteratively removed so the answer requires actually looking at the image.

## Summary

Published as "Seeing Beyond Words," MatVQA targets the visual half of materials expertise: reading microscopy and diffraction imagery and integrating it with multi-step scientific reasoning across structure, property, and performance. Its 1,325 questions span four structure-property-performance reasoning tasks and are generated by an automated pipeline (MArxivAgent) from the materials literature, with textual shortcuts iteratively removed so models cannot answer from captions alone. Benchmarking 17 open- and closed-source MLLMs reveals substantial gaps in current multimodal reasoning.

## Tasks

1,325 visual-scientific questions across four structure-property-performance reasoning tasks over real materials imagery (microscopy, diffraction); static multimodal QA. Generated via the MArxivAgent pipeline with shortcut removal.

## Domains

Materials science — visual characterization reasoning over microscopy and diffraction imagery, tied to structure-property-performance relationships.

## Evaluation

- Accuracy across the four SPP reasoning tasks over 17 MLLMs; shortcut removal enforces genuine visual reasoning.
- **Reported.** Substantial gaps in current multimodal reasoning capabilities; no single numeric headline in the abstract.

## Typical Duration

Single-turn multimodal questions; no interactive setting.

## Main Contribution

A shortcut-resistant visual reasoning benchmark for materials imagery — measuring whether MLLMs can reason from what they see, not from what a caption tells them.

## Key Design Ideas

- Iterative textual-shortcut removal forces reliance on the image.
- Structure-property-performance task split mirrors the materials reasoning chain.
- Automated literature-derived generation (MArxivAgent) scales question creation.

## Strengths

- Real microscopy and diffraction imagery, not synthetic figures.
- The anti-shortcut construction addresses a pervasive weakness in multimodal science benchmarks.
