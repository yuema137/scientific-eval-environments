# OpenXRD (2025)

> **English** | [简体中文](../zh/works/openxrd.md)

> **First appeared:** 2025-07-12 · **Source:** [arXiv initial submission](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09155)

## Overview

OpenXRD is a benchmark framework for LLM and multimodal-LLM X-ray diffraction question answering: 217 expert-curated crystallography questions evaluated across 74 state-of-the-art models under closed-book and open-book conditions, finding that expert-reviewed context helps mid-sized models most.

## Summary

XRD is the workhorse of crystallography, and OpenXRD tests whether foundation models understand it: 217 expert-curated XRD questions answered by 74 LLMs and MLLMs (7B–70B and beyond), in both closed-book form and open-book form with supporting passages. The passages are generated by GPT-4.5 and refined by crystallography experts, which sets up the headline finding — expert-reviewed materials give significantly larger gains than AI-generated ones even at matched token counts, and mid-sized models benefit most while very large models saturate or suffer interference.

## Tasks

217 expert-curated XRD/crystallography questions under closed-book and open-book conditions; static QA across 74 models. Whether inputs include real XRD-pattern images is not confirmed in the abstract.

## Domains

Materials science — crystallography: X-ray diffraction knowledge and reasoning.

## Evaluation

- Accuracy under closed-book vs. open-book conditions, comparing expert-refined vs. AI-generated context at matched token counts.
- **Reported.** Mid-sized models (7B–70B) gain the most from context; very large models saturate or show interference; expert-reviewed context beats AI-generated context.

## Typical Duration

Single-turn question answering; no interactive setting.

## Main Contribution

A crystallography-specific evaluation at unusual model breadth (74), with a controlled comparison isolating the value of expert-curated context over AI-generated context.

## Key Design Ideas

- Matched-token expert-vs-AI context comparison cleanly attributes gains to context quality.
- Closed- and open-book conditions separate parametric knowledge from retrieval use.
- 74-model coverage makes the mid-size-benefits-most finding robust.

## Strengths

- Venue-verified (Digital Discovery, RSC) with the widest model sweep among documented materials benchmarks.
- The context-quality result is directly actionable for retrieval-augmented materials assistants.

## Limitations
