It is included here as a reference paper, not a benchmark contribution.

## Summary

The paper argues LLMs are undergoing a paradigm shift from conversational generators toward integrated AI systems. It conceptualizes the shift along two dimensions: advancing LLMs toward more deliberate reasoning through inference-time computation and reflection; and progressing toward persistent workstation systems with reusable skills and state management rather than ad hoc tool usage.
