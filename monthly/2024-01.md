## What Changed This Month

### [Trajectory Evaluation](../topics/trajectory_evaluation.md)

Here the unit of judgment is no longer just the final answer. The trajectory itself becomes evidence about what the agent saw, decided, and missed.

- [AgentBoard](../works/agentboard.md): AgentBoard is a multi-turn LLM-agent evaluation benchmark paired with an analytical dashboard. Its central design commitment is that agent evaluation should not collapse to a binary success rate: performance is instead measured by a *fine-grained progress rate* over annotated subgoals in partially observable environments.
