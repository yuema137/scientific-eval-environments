## Month at a Glance

March 2024 is small but important because BrainBench brings a full discipline into scope without pretending that general QA is enough. The benchmark anchors neuroscience evaluation in expert-authored questions across molecular, systems, cognitive, and clinical levels, making field breadth part of the measurement problem.

## What Changed This Month

### [Scientific Agent Benchmarks](../topics/scientific_agents.md)

This group asks what counts as a defensible scientific benchmark once a field's artifacts, tools, and verification constraints stop looking like generic QA.

- [BrainBench](../works/brainbench.md): BrainBench is a forward-looking benchmark for neuroscience, introduced in the paper "Large language models surpass human experts in predicting neuroscience results": given an original abstract from the Journal of Neuroscience and a version altered to change the result while maintaining coherency, the model must identify which reports the real outcome. It is a static two-alternative task, not an agent benchmark (see the repository note under Limitations).
