# August 2024 Monthly Report

> **English** | [简体中文](../zh/monthly/2024-08.md)

> **Coverage:** First appearances during 2024-08

## Month at a Glance

August 2024 brings planning and execution closer to real operational settings. TransportBench, SimBench, BLADE, and PEOA all care about structured action under practical constraints, whether the agent is planning transport, running simulations, or reasoning over embodied tasks.

## What Changed This Month

### [Scientific Agent Benchmarks](../topics/scientific_agents.md)

This group asks what counts as a defensible scientific benchmark once a field's artifacts, tools, and verification constraints stop looking like generic QA.

- [BLADE](../works/blade.md): BLADE benchmarks language-model agents for data-driven science: 12 datasets paired with research questions drawn from scientific literature, with ground truth from independent analyses by expert data scientists, evaluating whether agents can integrate domain knowledge, statistics, and data understanding in open-ended analysis.
- [PEOA](../works/peoa.md): PEOA (Process Engineering Operations Assistant) is a modular LLM-agent framework for chemical and process engineering problem-solving that is released together with two custom benchmark datasets — MathComp and ChemProc — and a staged tool-learning evaluation protocol used to compare the framework against proprietary LLMs.
- [SimBench](../works/simbench.md): SimBench evaluates and diagnoses LLM-based digital-twin generation for multi-physics simulation: simulator-oriented LLMs build digital twins for the open-source Chrono simulator — multibody dynamics, finite element analysis, vehicle dynamics, robotic dynamics, and sensor simulation — through multi-turn interactions, scored by an LLM judge with predefined rules and human-in-the-loop guidance.
- [TransportBench](../works/transportbench.md): TransportBench is a dataset of 140 undergraduate transportation-engineering problems drawn from two University of Illinois civil-engineering courses, used with human-expert grading to measure the accuracy, answer consistency and reasoning behaviour of seven commercial and open-weight LLMs.

### [Trajectory Evaluation](../topics/trajectory_evaluation.md)

Here the unit of judgment is no longer just the final answer. The trajectory itself becomes evidence about what the agent saw, decided, and missed.

- [PEOA](../works/peoa.md): PEOA (Process Engineering Operations Assistant) is a modular LLM-agent framework for chemical and process engineering problem-solving that is released together with two custom benchmark datasets — MathComp and ChemProc — and a staged tool-learning evaluation protocol used to compare the framework against proprietary LLMs.

### [Skill Hierarchy](../topics/skill_hierarchy.md)

These works stop treating capability as a single score and instead separate the stack of subskills that a result depends on.

## Selected Topic Developments

[Scientific Agent Benchmarks](../topics/scientific_agents.md), [Trajectory Evaluation](../topics/trajectory_evaluation.md), [Skill Hierarchy](../topics/skill_hierarchy.md) stay at the center of this month, but the useful point is not the list itself. Read together, these topics show that scientific benchmarks become much more useful once they expose the intermediate evidence behind a result rather than only the result itself.
