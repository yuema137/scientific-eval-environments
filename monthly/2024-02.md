## Month at a Glance

February 2024 is the archive's first clean planning month. TravelPlanner and LoTa-Bench separate two questions that later stay distinct: can an agent satisfy real-world constraints while making a plan, and can that plan survive long embodied execution?

## What Changed This Month

### [General Long-Horizon Agent Benchmarks](../topics/long_horizon_evaluation.md)

These papers push on the same bottleneck from different sides: once an agent must keep state across many steps, the benchmark has to expose where progress stalls.

- [LoTa-Bench](../works/lota-bench.md): LoTa-Bench benchmarks language-oriented task planners for embodied agents with fully automated scoring: LLM-generated plans are executed in simulators and graded on goal satisfaction, over two dataset-simulator pairs — ALFRED on AI2-THOR and an extended Watch-And-Help on VirtualHome.
- [TravelPlanner](../works/travelplanner.md): TravelPlanner is a real-world planning benchmark with 1,225 curated travel intents, a closed sandbox containing nearly four million records, and tools for constructing multi-day itineraries under environment, commonsense, and user constraints.

### [Planning & Decision-Making Evaluation](../topics/planning_decision_evaluation.md)

These works look upstream of execution and ask whether the chosen next step was good at the moment of choice.

- [LoTa-Bench](../works/lota-bench.md): LoTa-Bench benchmarks language-oriented task planners for embodied agents with fully automated scoring: LLM-generated plans are executed in simulators and graded on goal satisfaction, over two dataset-simulator pairs — ALFRED on AI2-THOR and an extended Watch-And-Help on VirtualHome.
- [TravelPlanner](../works/travelplanner.md): TravelPlanner is a real-world planning benchmark with 1,225 curated travel intents, a closed sandbox containing nearly four million records, and tools for constructing multi-day itineraries under environment, commonsense, and user constraints.

## Selected Topic Developments

[General Long-Horizon Agent Benchmarks](../topics/long_horizon_evaluation.md), [Planning & Decision-Making Evaluation](../topics/planning_decision_evaluation.md) stay at the center of this month, but the useful point is not the list itself. The month is best read as separating two questions that are often confused: how long the task runs, and whether the agent chose the right next action.
