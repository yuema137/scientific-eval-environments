# August 2026 Monthly Report

> **English** | [简体中文](../zh/monthly/2026-08.md)

> **Coverage:** First appearances during 2026-08

## Month at a Glance

August 2026 is the densest month in the archive so far, with 39 first appearances, and it reads less like a single trend than like a field splitting into clearly differentiated subproblems. Scientific benchmarking remains the largest surface, but the real story is the coordination among three others: trajectory evaluation is becoming a serious diagnostic discipline, long-horizon benchmarking is broadening from task completion to agent improvement loops, and evaluator reliability is starting to be benchmarked in its own right.

This is also the month where the repository's "evaluation drives development" thesis becomes easiest to see in the literature itself. Several August works are not just asking whether an agent succeeds. They are asking whether the telemetry is sufficient, whether the replay is faithful, whether the judge can be trusted, whether the harness can improve itself, and whether a benchmark predicts anything beyond the benchmark.

## What Changed This Month

### [Scientific Agent Benchmarks](../topics/scientific_agents.md)

August's scientific benchmark work is no longer one thing. It breaks into at least two recognizable modes. One mode pushes benchmark construction deeper into real scientific or engineering workflows: [Agentic Self-Driving Microscopy Benchmarks](../works/agentic-microscopy-benchmarks.md), [AutoResearchEval](../works/autoresearcheval.md), [PACE-Bench](../works/pace-bench.md), [DSAgentBench](../works/dsagentbench.md), [CRAFTS](../works/crafts.md), [MMArch](../works/mmarch.md), [TCS-Bench](../works/tcs-bench.md), [EnergyBridge](../works/energybridge.md), and [BrainBench (EEG)](../works/brainbench-eeg.md) all widen the set of professional workflows being operationalized for evaluation.

A second mode is more diagnostic and sociotechnical. [Agents Catching Agents](../works/agents-catching-agents.md) asks whether committees of agents can be socially induced to adopt shortcut cues that clinicians would ignore. [Apodex Discovery](../works/apodex-discovery.md) scores discovery trajectories blind to outcome across six process dimensions. [Science Edge Evaluation (SEE)](../works/science-edge-evaluation.md), [Replica](../works/replica.md), [Fisher-R1 / P-Bench](../works/fisher-r1.md), and [onepot-Bench 0](../works/onepot-bench.md) each push on a slightly different question about realism, replicability, or benchmark design. [Position: AI Agents in Scientific Teams Should Be Studied as Human-Agent Systems](../works/ai-agents-in-scientific-teams.md) and [Co-Evolution in Agentic Systems: Toward Self-Directed Evolution Beyond Human Design](../works/co-evolution-agentic-systems-survey.md) sit outside benchmark construction narrowly defined, but they clarify why the surrounding human and organizational structure keeps showing up in the month's empirical work.

The practical reading is that August does not offer one new gold-standard benchmark. It shows the benchmark landscape fragmenting in a productive way: some evaluators care about domain workflow fidelity, some about discovery process, some about team dynamics, and some about whether the benchmark itself predicts anything outside its own test set.

### [Trajectory Evaluation](../topics/trajectory_evaluation.md)

Trajectory evaluation becomes a field in its own right this month. [AutoResearchEval](../works/autoresearcheval.md), [Beyond Final Scores](../works/beyond-final-scores.md), [SWE-RPG](../works/a-unified-issue-resolution-benchmark-for-requireme.md), [SearchAuditor](../works/searchauditor.md), [TRAJDEBUG](../works/trajdebug.md), [TelemetrySuffBench](../works/telemetrysuffbench.md), and [The Replay Gap](../works/the-replay-gap.md) are all trying to answer versions of the same question: what evidence about an agent run is sufficient to diagnose it, replay it, attribute credit, or compare it fairly?

What changes in August is that the literature starts distinguishing several subproblems that used to blur together. One line asks whether the trace is rich enough to support diagnosis at all. Another asks whether replayed or reconstructed traces preserve the original decision-making signal. A third asks whether stage-level or counterfactual analysis can separate planning quality from late execution failure. By this point, trajectory evaluation is not just "score intermediate steps." It is becoming the methodology for turning agent behavior into inspectable evidence.

### [General Long-Horizon Agent Benchmarks](../topics/long_horizon_evaluation.md)

Long-horizon work also changes character in August. [RoboGraphBench](../works/compiling-and-benchmarking-task-state-horizons-for.md) and [PATH-Bench](../works/path-bench.md) still care about the classical problem of maintaining relevant state across many steps, but [Evo-Bench](../works/evo-bench.md), [HarnessOpt-Bench](../works/harnessopt-bench.md), [R³-Bench](../works/r3-bench.md), [SkillMisevo-Bench](../works/skillmisevo-bench.md), [SWE-Bench ProMax](../works/swe-bench-promax.md), and [PACE-Bench](../works/pace-bench.md) make the scope wider. The long-horizon object is not only "finish the task"; it is increasingly "adapt the harness," "allocate resources across many decisions," or "improve a system over repeated cycles."

That is why August feels different from earlier long-horizon months. The horizon is no longer only in the task. It is also in the development loop around the task.

## Selected Topic Developments

August keeps [Scientific Agent Benchmarks](../topics/scientific_agents.md), [Trajectory Evaluation](../topics/trajectory_evaluation.md), and [General Long-Horizon Agent Benchmarks](../topics/long_horizon_evaluation.md) at the center, but this is also the month where adjacent pages such as [Evaluator Reliability & Validation](../topics/evaluator_reliability_validation.md), [Credit Assignment](../topics/credit_assignment.md), [Agent Harnesses & Scaffolding](../topics/agent_harnesses_scaffolding.md), and [Skill Learning & Evolution](../topics/skill_learning_evolution.md) start to pull their own weight inside the monthly story rather than acting as cross-references only.
