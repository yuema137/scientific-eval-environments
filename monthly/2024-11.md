# November 2024 Monthly Report

## Month at a Glance

November 2024 is small but methodologically mixed in a useful way. RE-Bench, OpenCATP, RoboSpatial, MaCBench, and EEE-Bench show evaluation reaching into budgets, embodied geometry, and coordinated manipulation, making resource limits and physical context harder to treat as afterthoughts.

## What Changed This Month

### [Scientific Agent Benchmarks](../topics/scientific_agents.md)

This group asks what counts as a defensible scientific benchmark once a field's artifacts, tools, and verification constraints stop looking like generic QA.

- [EEE-Bench](../works/eee-bench.md): EEE-Bench is a comprehensive multimodal electrical and electronics engineering benchmark: 2,860 curated problems spanning 10 essential subdomains (analog circuits, control systems, and more) that require understanding intricate images like abstract circuits and system diagrams alongside professional instructions — where 17 LLMs and LMMs average only 19.48%–46.78%.
- [MaCBench](../works/macbench.md): MaCBench probes the limitations of multimodal (vision-language) models for chemistry and materials research across three core aspects — data extraction, experimental understanding, and results interpretation — finding near-perfect equipment identification and standardized data extraction but fundamental limits in spatial reasoning, cross-modal synthesis, and multi-step inference.
- [RE-Bench](../works/re-bench.md): RE-Bench (Research Engineering Benchmark, v1) evaluates the frontier AI R&D capabilities of language-model agents against human experts: 7 open-ended ML research-engineering environments with reference solutions, plus data from 71 eight-hour attempts by 61 human experts — with agents outscoring humans 4x at a 2-hour budget but humans winning 2x by 32 hours.

### [Resource-aware Evaluation](../topics/resource_aware_evaluation.md)

These papers make efficiency part of the evaluation target, so success only counts when the agent reaches it without spending blindly.

- [CATP-LLM / OpenCATP](../works/catp-llm.md): CATP-LLM (Cost-Aware Tool Planning with LLMs) is a framework for empowering LLMs to plan tool use while accounting for tool execution costs, accompanied by OpenCATP, described as the first dataset for cost-aware planning (11,100 evaluation samples). It is documented here for OpenCATP, its cost-aware *evaluation* contribution; the paper's headline contribution — the CATP-LLM planning method — is agent-planning work adjacent to this repository's evaluation focus (see the repository note under Limitations).

### [Skill Hierarchy](../topics/skill_hierarchy.md)

These works stop treating capability as a single score and instead separate the stack of subskills that a result depends on.

- [MaCBench](../works/macbench.md): MaCBench probes the limitations of multimodal (vision-language) models for chemistry and materials research across three core aspects — data extraction, experimental understanding, and results interpretation — finding near-perfect equipment identification and standardized data extraction but fundamental limits in spatial reasoning, cross-modal synthesis, and multi-step inference.
